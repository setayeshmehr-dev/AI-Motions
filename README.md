# 🤟 Doppel 
A real-time 3D avatar app with **face-based authentication**, built with **Next.js, TypeScript, Three.js, and MediaPipe**.
Doppel turns your webcam into a full-body motion capture studio — your avatar mirrors your body, face, and emotions, live in the browser. No passwords. No servers.

## 🎯 Project Focus

- Face is the only password — biometric login with liveness detection
- Real-time full-body mirroring at ~60 FPS
- Emotional gestures and expression mapping
- 100% client-side — zero servers, zero data transmission
- Clean, consistent, and responsive interface

### 🔐 Face Authentication

Register with name, email and your face — then log in with your face alone. 478 landmarks are mapped into a 128-dimensional embedding, verified in about a second.

<img width="515" height="601" alt="Image" src="https://placehold.co/515x601/0A0713/A78BFA?text=Face+Auth" />

---

### 🧠 Liveness Detection

A 3-step challenge before any face is trusted: blink twice, turn your head, give a smile. Printed photos, screen replays and masks can't get through.

<img width="510" height="602" alt="Image" src="https://placehold.co/510x602/0A0713/A78BFA?text=Liveness+Check" />

---

### 🕺 Real-Time Body Mirroring

A 33-point skeleton mirrors your whole body live. Sit, stand, wave or lean — it moves when you move, at a smooth 60 FPS.

<img width="510" height="598" alt="Image" src="https://placehold.co/510x598/0A0713/A78BFA?text=Body+Tracking" />

### 😊 Facial Expressions

52 blendshapes and 478 face points tracked per frame — independent blinking, live lip-sync, and emotion mapping. It smiles when you smile.
